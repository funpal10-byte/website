Climate

A roadmap to low-carbon steel

AM/NS India is positioned as one of the country's least polluting steel producers, and is investing to cut emissions intensity even as it grows. The company is targeting a 20% reduction in CO₂ intensity by 2030 — to around 1.8 tonnes of CO₂ per tonne of crude steel — against a 2021 baseline, as a step towards a net-zero future.

  • Large-scale renewable energy — ~7 GW planned by 2032
  • Carbon Capture & Utilisation (CCU) technologies at new plants
  • Energy-efficient Basic Oxygen Furnace steelmaking & automation
  • Use of natural gas and cleaner process routes where feasible

Community impact

Steel that strengthens communities

AM/NS India's vision extends beyond the mill gate — empowering communities through employment and enriching millions of lives across the country through CSR programmes in education, healthcare, skill development, water and livelihoods. The company also publishes an annual Sustainability Report detailing its environmental, social and governance performance.
