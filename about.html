About AM/NS India

The story of how two of the world's leading steelmakers came together to build a flagship steel enterprise for India.

Overview

A flagship partnership for Indian steel

ArcelorMittal Nippon Steel India Limited (AM/NS India) is a 60:40 joint venture between ArcelorMittal — the world's leading steel and mining company — and Nippon Steel Corporation, one of the largest steel producers globally. The company was created in December 2019 following the joint acquisition of Essar Steel India.

Today AM/NS India is a leading integrated flat carbon steel producer, operating one of the world's largest single-location flat steel plants at Hazira in Gujarat, supported by pellet plants and captive port infrastructure on India's eastern coast. The company is in the midst of a transformational expansion to roughly double capacity and reduce its carbon footprint.

Our journey

Key milestones

From a landmark insolvency acquisition to one of India's most ambitious steel expansion programmes.

2019

The joint venture is born

ArcelorMittal and Nippon Steel complete the joint acquisition of Essar Steel India under India's Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code — the largest private-sector transaction to close in India that year. The company is renamed ArcelorMittal Nippon Steel India Limited on 16 December 2019.

2020 – 2021

Integration & stabilisation

The Hazira complex — with a crude steel capacity of around 9.6 million tonnes — is integrated and ramped up, and downstream assets and port infrastructure are consolidated under the new ownership.

2022

Expansion decision & infrastructure

The partners commit to major investment in upstream and hot-rolling facilities at Hazira, and agree to acquire associated port and power-related assets to secure raw-material and logistics capacity.

2024 – 2025

Value-added & automotive steel

AM/NS India commissions advanced auto-steel lines at Hazira — including India's first continuous galvanizing line capable of Advanced High-Strength Steel up to 1180 MPa — and acquires land for a new integrated mill in Andhra Pradesh.

2026 & beyond

Towards 15, 24 and 40 MTPA

The Hazira expansion targets 15 MTPA, with a roadmap to 24 MTPA by 2030 and a long-term vision of 40 MTPA — alongside a new greenfield mill at Rajayyapeta, Andhra Pradesh, and an ambitious renewable-energy programme.
