India-first technology

Advanced Automotive Steel

AM/NS India operates the country's first continuous galvanizing line capable of producing Advanced High-Strength Steel (AHSS) up to 1180 MPa — lighter, safer automotive bodies.

  • AHSS up to 1180 MPa
  • Continuous galvanizing (CGL) & annealing (CGAL) lines
  • Lightweighting for fuel efficiency & EVs
  • Dedicated 2 MTPA auto-focused cold rolling mill
Energy & infra

Pipes & Tubes

Line pipe and tubular products for the oil & gas, water and infrastructure sectors, manufactured to international standards.

  • API-grade line pipe
  • Structural & conveyance tubes
  • For pipelines, water & construction
Upstream raw material

Iron Ore Pellets

Significant pelletisation capacity from eastern-coast plants at Paradip and Vizag, supplying high-quality feed for integrated steelmaking.

  • High-grade iron ore pellets
  • Plants at Paradip (Odisha) & Vizag (Andhra Pradesh)
  • Captive port-linked logistics
  • Backward integration for cost & quality
