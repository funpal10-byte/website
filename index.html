Who we are

Two of the world's leading steelmakers, building in India

ArcelorMittal Nippon Steel India Limited (AM/NS India) was formed in December 2019 as a 60:40 joint venture between ArcelorMittal, the world's leading steel and mining company, and Nippon Steel, one of the world's largest steel producers, following the acquisition of Essar Steel India.

  • Leading integrated flat carbon steel producer in India
  • World-class Hazira plant — among the largest single-location flat steel mills globally
  • Iron-ore pellet plants and captive ports on India's eastern coast
  • Long-term vision to grow towards 40 MTPA of capacity

What we make

Where we operate

A pan-India footprint, anchored at Hazira

Our integrated steel complex at Hazira in Gujarat is the heart of AM/NS India — supported by pellet plants, downstream units and captive port infrastructure across western and eastern India, plus a major new greenfield mill rising in Andhra Pradesh.

  • Hazira, Gujarat — integrated steel mill & ongoing 9→15 MTPA expansion
  • Paradip, Odisha — pellet plant & captive deep-water jetty
  • Vizag, Andhra Pradesh — pellet plant & deep-draft port terminal
  • Pune, Maharashtra — downstream processing base
  • Rajayyapeta, Andhra Pradesh — new integrated greenfield mill
