Our Operations

A pan-India footprint spanning integrated steelmaking, iron-ore pelletisation, downstream processing and captive port infrastructure.

Where we operate

Plants & infrastructure across India

Anchored by Hazira in the west and pellet-and-port complexes in the east, with a major greenfield mill underway in the south.

9 MTPAIntegrated mill

Hazira Steel Complex

Surat district · Gujarat · Western India

The heart of AM/NS India — one of the largest single-location flat steel plants in the world, producing the full range of hot and cold rolled, coated and value-added steel. A landmark expansion is underway to grow capacity from 9 to 15 MTPA by 2026, with a roadmap to 24 MTPA by 2030 using technologies such as Basic Oxygen Furnace steelmaking and advanced automation. The site also hosts new auto-steel lines, including India's first CGL for AHSS up to 1180 MPa.

Pellets+ Captive port

Paradip Pellet Plant & Jetty

Paradip · Odisha · Eastern India

An iron-ore pellet plant on India's eastern coast, served by a dedicated deep-water jetty at Paradip Port and a captive conveyor for efficient pellet shipments. The complex also hosts a new Learning & Development Centre for employee capability building, and is being developed with sustainability features including carbon-capture and zero-liquid-discharge systems.

Pellets+ Port terminal

Vizag Pellet Plant & Terminal

Visakhapatnam · Andhra Pradesh · Eastern India

A pellet plant integrated with a deep-draft ore terminal at Visakhapatnam Port, connected by conveyor for seamless raw-material handling. This eastern-coast hub strengthens AM/NS India's backward integration and logistics for high-quality pellet feed.

Down-stream base

Pune Downstream Facility

Pune · Maharashtra · Western India

A downstream processing and manufacturing base serving customers in western India, adding value close to key automotive and engineering markets.

NewGreenfield

Rajayyapeta Integrated Steel Mill

Rajayyapeta · Andhra Pradesh · Southern India

A major new greenfield integrated steel mill under development in Andhra Pradesh, with a planned multi-million-tonne crude steel capacity. Land acquisition and early works are advancing as part of AM/NS India's long-term growth towards a vision of 40 MTPA nationally.
